# Bookmark Design System

## Intent
The Bookmark design should feel calm, focused, and distraction-free to encourage reading.

## Colors
- **Primary:** Use `#1D4ED8` for primary buttons and progress bars.
- **Background:** Use `#F1F5F9` to reduce eye strain.
- **Text:** Use `#0F172A` for high contrast readability.
